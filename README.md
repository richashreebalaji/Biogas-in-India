# The Role of Biogas in India

An undergraduate group project exploring
biogas as a waste-to-energy solution in India
and its environmental, economic and social
implications.

## Project Overview

This project examines the biogas ecosystem in
India, from organic waste collection and
processing to energy production and distribution.

## Methodology

- Field visit to the Carbon Masters and Hasiru
  Dala Innovations biogas plant in Harohalli.
- Interviews with industry stakeholders,
  including GPS Renewables.
- Stakeholder mapping and analysis.
- Review of government policies, business
  models and secondary literature.

## Key Insights

- Biogas connects waste management with
  renewable energy production.
- Large-scale biogas production requires
  significant capital investment and a
  reliable supply of organic waste.
- The sector offers multiple revenue streams,
  including fuel and organic fertilisers.
- Power and economic benefits are unevenly
  distributed across stakeholders.

## Areas Explored

- Renewable energy
- Circular economy
- Waste management
- Stakeholder analysis
- Environmental justice
- Business models and policy
